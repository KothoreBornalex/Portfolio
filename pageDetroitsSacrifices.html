    Pitch
    Detroit's Sacrifices is a student project made to learn how to code (from ground zero) and build a game in Unity. In this project I discovered foundational concepts: functions, arrays, variables, and game architecture.
    Player
    My player controller is inspired by classic Hitman and Payday 2 mechanics.

    It allows players to take a stealthy approach using crouch, walking, and hacking, or brute force their way through encounters with assault rifle combat and sprint abilities.
    AI And Boss Fight
    I created 2 AI systems: a soldier AI that patrols and guards zones for stealth/combat scenarios, and a dynamic boss AI inspired by Sekiro that pursues the player with stylish combat patterns.
    Hacking In The Matrix
    After selecting an interactable object to hack, the player enters the matrix cyberspace and triggers a minigame to complete the hack.
