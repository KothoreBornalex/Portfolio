Project Overview

Woolsome is a charming co-op top-down adventure created by an 11-person student team. Two players work together to lead, guide, and protect their flock through dangerous mountain trails.

I focused on core C++ player mechanics, flock herding behaviors, physics interaction pipelines, and designer camera tooling in Unreal Engine 5.

Herding Mechanics & Interactions

Developed the flocking and herding algorithms that govern sheep movement, fear responses, and attraction toward players.

Implemented bite actions to clear wooden debris, lantern mechanics for cave navigation, and co-op puzzle pressure switches.

Physics Grabbing & Spline Camera

Built a physics-driven grab and throw system allowing players to carry sheep across chasms or toss boulders to solve environmental puzzles.

Created a spline-based framing camera tool that automatically interpolates framing, zoom, and pitch to keep both co-op players comfortably in view at all times.
