    Pitch
    With your friend, lead your flock through idyllic environments. Avoid obstacles, solve problems and protect from danger.

    Ascend the mountains and reach the end of your journey where summer reigns eternally, where you and your flock shall find peace. This is a co-op only title (2 players required).
    Bite, Interact
    Lead your sheep in the right direction and herd them away from danger.

    Bite enemies and clear your path. Light torches and shine through obscurity.
