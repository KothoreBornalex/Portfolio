Performance & Engineering

Project Overview

Wandering Traveller is a mobile title developed to explore mobile rendering limits and real-time crowd rendering on constrained smartphone hardware.

We engineered custom memory architectures and rendering techniques to achieve desktop-level fidelity while preserving battery life and a steady framerate.

Optimization Strategy

1. Zero-Allocation Object Pooling: Eliminated Garbage Collection pauses by pre-allocating pools for projectiles, damage numbers, and audio cues.

2. GPU Vertex Animation: Integrated custom texture-baked animation tools to bypass the CPU skinning bottleneck completely.

Performance Benchmark Results

Before Optimization: 30 animated skeletal AI characters caused heavy CPU stalls, resulting in 16–25 FPS with noticeable thermal throttling.

After Optimization: Framerate surged to a rock-solid 60 FPS with 112+ simultaneous active AI units on screen.

Core Gameplay & Progression

Developed scalable character stat components, responsive touch controls, dynamic day/night atmospheric transitions, and procedural wave progression algorithms.
