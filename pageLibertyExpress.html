Project Overview

Liberty Express is a narrative FPS set aboard a speeding train under relentless assault by the Sheriff's drone army. Players follow the escape of outlaw Jack "The Revolver".

Working in a team of 8, I was responsible for core gameplay mechanics in C++, weapon feedback loops, camera feel, and designer tooling in Unreal Engine 5.

Responsive Player Controller

Engineered a responsive first-person character controller balancing classic Western gunfighting with fast-paced mobility.

Implemented precise hitscan mechanics, dynamic recoil curves, kinetic reload animations, and immersive camera feedback to elevate the punchy feel of revolver duels.

Designer Tooling & Data Architecture

To streamline balancing across the team, I exposed weapon parameters, camera spring settings, and damage falloff to designers through Primary Data Assets.

This decoupled game logic from content tweaking, allowing game designers to iterate on combat feel and test balancing values instantly during playtests.
