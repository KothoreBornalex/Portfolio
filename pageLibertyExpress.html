    Liberty Express
    2024 - PC - FPS - Unreal Engine 5 - 1 Month - 8 Workers
    Gameplay Programming - Feedback - Tooling
    Pitch
    Liberty Express is a narrative FPS where you follow the escape of Jack "the Revolver" aboard a train besieged on all sides by the Sheriff's police drones.
    Player Controller
    The player controller is designed to blend elements of the Old West with futuristic technology. It offers smooth, responsive movement with precision aiming, optimized for fast-paced gunplay.
    The controller handles classic Western-style gunfighting, including revolver handling, fast reload animations, and hand-to-hand combat.
    Tooling
    To make it easier for the Game Designers to balance and adjust game feel, I made weapon data, player stats, and camera settings fully customizable.
    I used Primary Data Assets to enable rapid iteration and modular parameter tweaking in Unreal Engine.
